Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about T-Minus Dashboard, in one place.

What is T-Minus Dashboard?

T-Minus Dashboard is a personal, customizable countdown and life-tracking board. Instead of one fixed layout, you build your own board out of widgets — a live clock, a calendar, one-time or recurring countdowns, progress bars, checklists, and more — arranged the way that makes sense for your own schedule, goals, or milestones.

Do I need to create an account?

No. T-Minus Dashboard works entirely in your browser. There's no sign-up, no login, and no personal information required to use it.

Where is my dashboard saved?

Your widgets, settings, and layout are saved locally in your own browser. That means your dashboard will be there the next time you visit on the same device and browser, but it isn't stored on our servers or tied to an account. If you switch browsers or devices, you'll need to rebuild your dashboard there.

What's the difference between a one-time and a recurring countdown?

A one-time countdown counts down to a single fixed date, like a graduation or a trip. A recurring countdown counts down to the next occurrence of something that repeats — like a weekly appointment — and automatically rolls forward to the next one once the date passes.

Can I change how my dashboard looks?

Yes. T-Minus Dashboard includes multiple display styles you can switch between, so your board can look the way you want it to.

Is my data private?

Yes. We don't collect names, emails, or personal information, and your dashboard configuration stays in your own browser rather than being sent to us. Full details are in our Privacy Policy.

Is T-Minus Dashboard free?

Yes, T-Minus Dashboard is free to use.

How do I get in touch?

You can reach us anytime at countdown@tminusdashboard.com.
