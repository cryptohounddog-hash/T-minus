Privacy Policy

Effective date: July 12, 2026

T-Minus Dashboard ("we," "our," or "the site") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information is collected when you visit tminusdashboard.com, how it's used, and the choices available to you.

Information We Collect

T-Minus Dashboard is designed to work without requiring an account, sign-up, or personal information. The only information the site stores is:

  • Your dashboard configuration (which widgets you've added — clocks, countdowns, progress bars, checklists, and similar tools — along with their settings, sizes, and arrangement, and your chosen display style) — saved locally in your own browser using localStorage. This information stays on your device; it is not sent to us or stored on any server we control.

We do not collect names, email addresses, or any other personally identifiable information through the core functionality of this site.

Cookies and Third-Party Advertising

This site may use Google AdSense to display advertising. As part of this, Google and its partners use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this website or other websites.

  • Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to you based on your visit to this site and/or other sites on the internet.
  • You may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google's Ads Settings.
  • You may also opt out of a third-party vendor's use of cookies for personalized advertising by visiting www.aboutads.info.
  • If you are located in the European Economic Area, United Kingdom, or Switzerland, you will be shown a cookie consent notice that lets you choose whether to allow personalized advertising cookies before they are set.

Third-party vendors, including Google, may also use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this website or other websites.

Children's Privacy

T-Minus Dashboard is not directed at children under the age of 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us so we can address it.

Your Rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights under applicable privacy laws (such as the GDPR in the EU/UK or state privacy laws in the US) to access, correct, or request deletion of information collected about you. Because T-Minus Dashboard does not collect personal information on our own servers, most such requests would be handled directly through Google's own privacy tools linked above.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.
